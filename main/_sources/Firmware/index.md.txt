## Firmware workflow

1. Contact [ifm support](mailto:support.efector.object-ident@ifm.com) for firmware availability and the compatible ifm3d and ifmVisionAssistant versions for your OVP930 or OVP931.
2. Save the current VPU configuration before updating.
3. Follow the [firmware update instructions](fw_update.md) using ETH0 with a static IP address. Do not interrupt power or the Ethernet connection during the update.
4. After the VPU reboots, check the installed firmware version, review the configuration, and verify camera acquisition.
