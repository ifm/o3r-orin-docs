# 3D camera configuration

The O3R335 3D imager uses time-of-flight (ToF) measurements to provide distance, amplitude, and point cloud data.
The following table summarizes its specifications.

| Article | Image resolution 3D [px] | Angle of aperture [°] | Framerate [Hz] |
| ------- | ------------------------ | --------------------- | -------------- |
| O3R335  | 240 × 164                | 105° × 78°            | 20             |

## Configuration and data

- [States and triggering](triggering.md#states) — acquisition states, free-running operation, and software triggering of connected camera heads.
- [Modes](modes.md) — measurement ranges and their characteristics.
- [Acquisition parameters](AcquisitionParams/index_acquisition_params.md) — frame rate, exposure, delay, software trigger groups, offset, and channel settings.
- [Processing parameters](ProcessingParams/index_processing_params.md) — filters for ToF data and CUDA point cloud processing.
- [Images](ImagesDescription/index_images_description.md) — available image types, point clouds, and confidence information.
- [Intrinsic calibration](IntrinsicCalib/intrinsic_calib_3d.md) — optical models and calibration parameters.
