# Integration guides

Each implementation of the O3R platform will be unique and require different levels of integration. The following guides help you through the various aspects of integrating the O3R — the VPU and its connected camera heads — into your application, and ensure that you have all the necessary information to do so successfully.

For camera placement, see the [O3R335 clearance definition and illustrations](Mechanical/mechanical_guide.md#o3r335) and the [mounting examples](Mechanical/mechanical_guide.md#examples) in the mechanical guide.
